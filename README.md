ExpenseLog is a simple personal expense tracker that helps you log daily spending, see where your money goes, and stay on top of your budget — all right in your browser.

- ➕ **Add Expenses** — Log amount, category, date, and a short note
- 📋 **Expense List** — View all your expenses, newest first, with an easy delete option
- 📊 **Category Breakdown** — Visual chart showing how much you spend per category
- 📈 **Summary Stats** — See totals for today, this week, and this month at a glance
- 🔍 **Filters** — Filter expenses by category or date range
- 💾 **Auto-Save** — All data is saved in your browser, so it stays even after you refresh

- HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Browser localStorage for saving data — no backend or database required

1. Download or clone this repository
2. Open `index.html` in your browser
3. Start adding your expenses

1. Fill in the amount, category, date, and note for an expense
2. Click **Add Expense** — it appears in your list instantly
3. Check the summary stats and chart to see your spending breakdown
4. Use filters to view expenses by category or time period
5. Delete any entry you no longer need
